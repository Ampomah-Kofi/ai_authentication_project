Participation Voluntary

You are invited to take part in a usable security and privacy research study about authentication and account permissions in the AI era. The study is led by Kofi Ampomah, a doctoral student in the UA SPECTRAL Lab (Department of Computer Science, The University of Alabama), supervised by Dr. Le Nhat Tu. Being in this study is optional.

Please imagine these are your own accounts and act as you normally would. The sign-in and AI permission screens are a realistic simulation for research. They are not connected to any real account, and you will never enter a real password.

Some elements of the task are designed for research purposes and may not represent typical app behavior.

What you will do

You will complete a short interactive task that looks like signing in to an AI-enabled app and reviewing an account permission screen, then answer a brief recall question. At the end, you will receive an anonymous code to carry into the survey.

Your task

You want TaskFlow AI Assistant to help organize your class schedule and recent study files. Please decide as you normally would when you review the access request.

What we record

  • Which screen you are on and how long you spend there.
  • Which buttons you select during the simulated authentication and permission flow.
  • General interaction patterns during the task.
  • Your allow/cancel choice and your answers to the recall question.

We do not record your name, real passwords, or any real account credentials. The fields in the task are fake and are not submitted to any login service.

Privacy, risks, and choice

The task is anonymous. The main possible risk is mild discomfort from being asked to make decisions in a realistic authentication and permission-sharing simulation. You can stop at any time by closing the page.

Questions

Study contacts: Kofi Ampomah, kampomah@crimson.ua.edu, or Kunal Sarna, ksarna@crimson.ua.edu. Concerns about your rights as a participant: UA Office of Research Compliance, 1-877-820-3066, rscompliance@ua.edu.
